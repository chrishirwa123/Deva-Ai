Local-First & Private

Your AI. Your machine. Your data.

Deva AI's core chat runs on Ollama, on your own hardware. There's no required cloud subscription and no mandatory data upload Deva only reaches the internet when you explicitly ask it to research something.

Capabilities

Built for real, everyday use

Offline Conversations

Chat with Deva without an internet connection, once your local model is pulled through Ollama.

Personal Memory

Tell Deva to remember specific facts, review and edit them anytime, or ask it to forget something.

Online Research

When you're connected, ask Deva to research a topic it searches, reads, summarizes, and cites sources.

Knowledge Library

Upload PDFs, notes, and docs. Deva retrieves relevant excerpts instead of loading everything into every prompt.

JARVIS-Style Assistant

A dedicated voice-and-text assistant page with an animated status orb and browser speech support.

Full Owner Control

Configure the model, temperature, appearance and privacy settings and export or wipe your data anytime.

How It Works

Three moving parts, one experience

1

Ollama runs the model

Your existing local model (qwen3:1.7b) handles every response no data leaves your machine for ordinary chat.

2

Deva adds context

Relevant memories and knowledge excerpts are retrieved locally and added to the prompt before Ollama replies.

3

You stay in control

Research only runs when you ask, memories only save when you approve, and everything is exportable or erasable.
