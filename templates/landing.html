How It Works

          
Three moving parts, one experience

        


        

        

          

            

            
1

            
Ollama runs the model

            

              Your existing local model (qwen3:1.7b) handles every response. No
              data leaves your machine for ordinary chat.
            

          

          

            

            
2

            
Deva adds context

            

              Relevant memories and knowledge excerpts are retrieved locally and
              added to the prompt before Ollama replies.
            

          

          

            

            
3

            
You stay in control

            

              Research only runs when you ask, memories only save when you
              approve, and everything is exportable or erasable.
